The queen is the most powerful and most visible piece on the board. This chapter is the
project's visual layer — how the engine's thinking is made into something you can watch.

## Making thought visible

It covers turning a plain **pygame GUI** into an interactive sandbox: rendering the
search as a **node-pruning tree** with beta-cutoffs highlighted and unsearched paths
fading away, and projecting **neural attention heatmaps** straight onto the board.
