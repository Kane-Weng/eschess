The bishop sees the board along the diagonals — a different geometry from everything
else. This chapter is about teaching the engine to see positions differently too:
moving from handcrafted evaluation to learned evaluation.

## Learning to evaluate

It transitions from static **piece-square tables** to deep **convolutional / ResNet**
networks: a **policy network** that predicts promising moves and a **value network**
that scores positions. Those nets guide a **Monte Carlo Tree Search**, trained in an
**AlphaZero-style** self-play reinforcement-learning loop.
